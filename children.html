The heart of the initiative

Every child should be met with dignity, care, and possibility.

100 Children is IRF’s focused space for child support within its wider commitment to stronger families and communities.

The initiative reflects the foundation’s belief that reaching one life can help transform a family—and transformed families can strengthen communities.

What comes first

A child-centered foundation

The initiative is presented through three commitments consistent with IRF’s purpose.

01

Dignity & safety

Children should be treated with respect, care, and attention to their wellbeing and privacy.

02

Care & encouragement

Positive support can strengthen confidence, hope, and a child’s sense of possibility.

03

Opportunity

Children thrive when families and communities help open paths for learning and development.
