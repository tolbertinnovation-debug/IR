Our story

A foundation born from compassion—and built for lasting change.

ImpactReach Foundation was founded by Liberian humanitarian Michael Whea Mardeh after witnessing the struggles of the less privileged and those battling drug addiction.

His passion to restore dignity, create opportunity, and transform vulnerable lives led him to establish IRF—bringing hope, empowerment, and lasting impact to individuals, families, and communities.

Our approach

Change works best when communities shape it.

IRF centers people as capable partners. We listen, respond with dignity, and focus on opportunities that can grow beyond a single moment of support.

“When we reach one life, we transform a family. When we transform a family, we change a community.”
  1. 1

    Listen

    Start with people, their lived experience, and community priorities.

  2. 2

    Act with dignity

    Respond with care, respect, and practical support.

  3. 3

    Build opportunity

    Strengthen skills, livelihoods, relationships, and resilience.

  4. 4

    Grow lasting change

    Work with communities and partners toward stronger futures.
