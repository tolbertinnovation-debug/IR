Who we are

A foundation built to restore dignity and expand opportunity.

ImpactReach Foundation (IRF) is a Liberian humanitarian and development organization committed to improving lives through empowerment, education, rehabilitation, and sustainable development programmes.

IRF was founded by Liberian humanitarian Michael Whea-Mardeh after witnessing the struggles of the less privileged and those battling drug addiction. His passion to restore dignity, create opportunity, and transform vulnerable lives led him to establish IRF.

Mission

Empower vulnerable individuals and communities through education, skills, rehabilitation, and sustainable livelihoods.

Vision

A Liberia where every vulnerable person can learn, grow, participate, and build a dignified future.

Our approach

Identify. Innovate. Implement. Measure. Scale.

IRF works with communities to understand needs, design relevant responses, implement responsibly, measure progress, and grow solutions that demonstrate value.

“When we reach one life, we transform a family. When we transform a family, we change a community.”
  1. 1

    Identify

    Listen to communities and understand the real barriers people face.

  2. 2

    Innovate

    Design practical, locally relevant responses with dignity at the center.

  3. 3

    Implement

    Deliver with clear roles, responsible partnerships, and community participation.

  4. 4

    Measure

    Track learning, outcomes, and evidence rather than relying on assumptions.

  5. 5

    Scale

    Strengthen and expand approaches that demonstrate sustainable value.
