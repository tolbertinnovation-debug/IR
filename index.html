Who we are

A foundation built to restore dignity and expand opportunity.

ImpactReach Foundation (IRF) is a Liberian humanitarian and development organization committed to expanding opportunity through education, skills development, recovery support, and sustainable livelihoods.

IRF was founded by Liberian humanitarian Pastor Michael Whea Mardeh after witnessing the challenges faced by underserved communities and people affected by drug addiction. His commitment to dignity and opportunity led him to establish the foundation.

Mission

Empower vulnerable individuals and communities through education, skills, rehabilitation, and sustainable livelihoods.

Vision

A Liberia where every vulnerable person can learn, grow, participate, and build a dignified future.
