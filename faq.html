About the foundation

Purpose, leadership, and values

What is ImpactReach Foundation?

ImpactReach Foundation (IRF) is a Liberia-based humanitarian and development organization focused on dignity, opportunity, empowerment, and sustainable community development.

When was IRF founded?

IRF was founded on May 30, 2025, after Pastor Michael Whea Mardeh witnessed the struggles of vulnerable people and those affected by drug addiction.

Who founded and leads IRF?

Pastor Michael Whea Mardeh is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer. His leadership experience includes ministry, education, entrepreneurship, financial management, and community-based institution building.

Who leads Monitoring & Evaluation at IRF?

Fululai K. S. Mulbah is IRF’s Director of Monitoring & Evaluation. He provides strategic leadership in data management, research, program monitoring, evaluation, and evidence-based decision-making. Meet the leadership team.

Is IRF a Christian organization?

IRF’s approach to service is influenced by Christian values of compassion, integrity, stewardship, dignity, and hope. The foundation seeks to work respectfully with diverse partners and communities.

02
Programs & initiative

How IRF intends to serve

What are IRF’s program areas?

IRF presents five connected pathways: Youth Skills & Employment; Women & Widow Empowerment; Poverty Reduction & Livelihoods; Drug Prevention & Rehabilitation Support; and Family Strengthening & Child Support.

Are all programs currently accepting participants?

Current activity, location, eligibility, and availability should be confirmed directly with IRF. The website does not claim that every program is active or accepting participants at all times.

What is “100 Children, One Greater Future”?

It is an IRF flagship initiative intended to mobilize support for vulnerable children and help address educational needs such as school fees, uniforms and clothing, books, learning materials, and other essential needs.

How will children be selected for the initiative?

The eligibility and selection process has not yet been published. IRF will share those details after the process is confirmed and approved for public release.

03
Partnership & support

Ways to move the mission forward

How can an organization partner with IRF?

Contact IRF to discuss the shared purpose, intended contribution, responsibilities, safeguarding, accountability, communication, and next steps for a possible collaboration.

How can I support IRF?

You can express interest in financial support, in-kind contributions, volunteering, professional expertise, or partnership. Use the Donation page for a giving inquiry, or contact IRF directly for current and verified instructions.

Does the website publish payment details?

No official payment instructions are currently published on the website. Request and verify contribution information using an email address displayed on the IRF website before sending support.

Can I volunteer my time or professional skills?

Yes, you may contact IRF with your area of interest, availability, relevant experience, and the kind of support you hope to offer. Opportunities will depend on current needs and confirmation by the foundation.

Contact & updates

Verified information and communication

Where is IRF located?

ImpactReach Foundation is based in Monrovia, Liberia. An exact public street address and visiting hours have not been published. Arrange and confirm any in-person meeting by email before visiting.

How do I contact the foundation?

Email impactreachfoundation@gmail.com. You may also use the inquiry form on the Contact page, which prepares a message in your own email application.

Where can I find IRF news and results?

Verified program updates, results, community stories, and announcements will appear on the News & Stories and Our Impact pages after the information and permissions are confirmed.

How can I verify information that is not on the website?

Contact IRF through a published email address. For programs, contributions, partnerships, or visits, request current written information before making a commitment.
