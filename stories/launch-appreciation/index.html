AnnouncementNovember 10, 2026

Thank you for believing in the vision.

A message of appreciation following the Official Launch Ceremony of ImpactReach Foundation (IRF), and a call for partnership and collaboration.

On behalf of ImpactReach Foundation (IRF), I wish to express our heartfelt appreciation to our distinguished Keynote Speaker, Hon. Jackson K. George Jr., Executive Director of the Joseph Nyuma Boakai Foundation, for delivering such an inspiring and impactful message at our historic launch.

Our sincere gratitude also goes to all government institutions, ministries, agencies, honorable officials, distinguished guests, partners, supporters, and everyone who took the time to stand with us on this remarkable occasion.

Your presence, encouragement, and commitment have given greater meaning to our vision of Reaching Lives and Transforming Futures.

A call for partnership and collaboration

As we move forward, we warmly invite government institutions, development partners, NGOs, corporate organizations, philanthropists, and individuals to join hands with ImpactReach Foundation. We believe meaningful change is achieved through collaboration, shared responsibility, and collective action.

Whether through strategic partnerships, technical support, funding, resources, or community engagement, there is a place for everyone to contribute to this vision.

Let us move beyond conversations and turn shared commitments into measurable impact. Together, we can create opportunities, empower communities, and build a brighter future for generations to come.

The launch was only the beginning. The real journey starts with partnership, collaboration, and action!

Thank you for believing in the vision and becoming part of this journey.

IMPACTREACH FOUNDATION (IRF)Reaching Lives. Transforming Futures.
