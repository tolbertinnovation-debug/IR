Editorial commitments

Every story should protect trust.

IRF’s public storytelling should be accurate, dignified, clear about evidence, and respectful of privacy.

  1. 01

    Verify first

    Confirm names, dates, places, activities, and results before publication.

  2. 02

    Seek permission

    Use personal stories and images only with appropriate consent.

  3. 03

    Preserve dignity

    Present people as capable individuals, never as objects of pity.

  4. 04

    Show context

    Explain what happened without exaggeration or misleading claims.

Prepare a verified story

Four items complete every story card.

  • 1
    Approved imageA clear, respectful photograph with permission to publish.
  • 2
    Accurate titleA concise headline that reflects what actually happened.
  • 3
    Publication dateThe verified date the update is released.
  • 4
    Approved excerptA short summary with honest context and no unsupported claims.
