Who we are

A foundation built to reach lives—and help transform futures.

ImpactReach Foundation is a Liberian humanitarian and development organization committed to dignity, opportunity, empowerment, and lasting change.

How IRF began

Compassion became a commitment.

IRF was founded on May 30, 2025 by Liberian humanitarian Michael Whea Mardeh, after witnessing the struggles of the less privileged and those battling drug addiction.

His passion to restore dignity, create opportunity, and transform vulnerable lives led him to establish ImpactReach Foundation—bringing hope, empowerment, and lasting impact to individuals, families, and communities.

The foundation’s identity is rooted in a simple belief: meaningful change begins when people are seen, respected, and given a genuine opportunity to move forward.

Our purpose

A clear reason for every action

IRF’s direction grows from the founding commitment to restore dignity, create opportunity, and strengthen vulnerable lives.

Our mission

Restore dignity and create opportunity.

To bring compassionate, community-centered support that helps individuals, families, and communities move toward stronger futures.

Our vision

Hopeful, resilient communities.

A future where people have the dignity, support, and opportunity to shape better lives for themselves, their families, and their communities.
