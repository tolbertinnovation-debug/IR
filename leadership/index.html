Operations

Operations & coordination

Amb. Cyrus S. Kettor

Operations Director

Amb. Cyrus S. Kettor is a Liberian youth leader and humanitarian whose work centers on education, leadership, and community development.

As Operations Director, he coordinates day-to-day activities and supports programs for children and families.

He also serves as President of the Youth Department at The Power of His Word Ministries (City of Life) and is studying aviation, procurement, and logistics.

Finance

Finance & stewardship

Agnes W. Smith

Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

Agnes W. Smith brings experience in sociology, management, nursing, and psychological counseling. She holds a BSc in Sociology and Management and an Associate of Arts degree, and is a Licensed Practical Nurse.

As Chief Financial Officer, she supports budgeting, financial accountability, and responsible use of resources.

Her interest in education, wellness, and leadership development informs her commitment to children and families.

Monitoring & Evaluation

Evidence & learning

Fululai K. S. Mulbah

Director of Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E)

Fululai K. S. Mulbah is a Liberian statistician, data analyst, researcher, and entrepreneur.

As Director of Monitoring & Evaluation, he guides research, data management, program monitoring, and evaluation to help IRF learn from its work and make informed decisions.

He is committed to using knowledge and research to support individual opportunity and national development.
