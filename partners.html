Work with IRF

Local insight. Shared purpose. Greater reach.

IRF welcomes partnership conversations with people and organizations committed to dignified, lasting change in Liberia.

Why partnership matters

Complex challenges require connected action.

IRF brings local purpose and community-centered program direction. The right partners can bring knowledge, relationships, resources, and shared accountability.

Together, partners can explore practical ways to strengthen skills, livelihoods, recovery support, families, children, and community resilience.

Who we welcome

Different partners. One shared commitment.

IRF welcomes conversations across four broad partnership groups.

01

Development partners

Organizations interested in humanitarian and development collaboration.

02

Community leaders

People with local knowledge, trusted relationships, and community priorities.

03

Institutions

Educational, civic, health, faith-based, and professional institutions exploring shared work.

04

Donors & volunteers

Individuals interested in responsibly supporting IRF’s mission and programs.

How we want to work together

Partnership should strengthen trust—not just activity.

IRF’s partnership approach is designed around respect for people, community context, clarity, and responsible reporting.

01

Mutual respect

Each partner brings knowledge and value.

02

Community voice

Local priorities remain visible in decisions.

03

Clear expectations

Roles, resources, and responsibilities are discussed openly.

04

Integrity

Claims and public recognition are verified before publication.

A simple starting process

From first conversation to shared direction

Every partnership can begin with four clear steps.

  1. 1

    Connect

    Introduce your organization, interest, and contact details.

  2. 2

    Explore alignment

    Discuss shared purpose and the relevant program area.

  3. 3

    Define the work

    Clarify roles, scope, resources, and expectations.

  4. 4

    Learn together

    Review progress, evidence, and lessons responsibly.
