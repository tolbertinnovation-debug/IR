Responsible support

Trust should guide every contribution.

Clear purpose, verified information, dignity, and honest communication are essential to responsible support.

  1. 01

    Confirm the need

    Discuss current priorities before sending funds, goods, or services.

  2. 02

    Verify instructions

    Use a published IRF email to confirm current contribution details.

  3. 03

    Clarify purpose

    Agree on the program, intended use, or collaboration being supported.

  4. 04

    Protect dignity

    Keep people, consent, and respectful representation at the center.

Support questions

Helpful answers before you connect.

These answers reflect the information currently published on the IRF website.

Can I donate directly through this website?

No online donation or payment method has been published here. Contact IRF to request and verify current giving information.

Can I volunteer with IRF?

You can share your interest, experience, availability, and location. IRF can then confirm whether a suitable opportunity is available.

Can I send equipment or other items?

Contact IRF before sending anything so the need, quantity, delivery arrangements, and intended use can be discussed.

How can an organization support IRF?

Visit the Partners page or introduce your organization, the program area of interest, and the collaboration you would like to explore.
