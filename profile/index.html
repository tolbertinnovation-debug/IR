About the foundation

Moving people from vulnerability toward dignity and self-reliance.

ImpactReach Foundation (IRF) is a Liberia-based organization established to reach vulnerable and underserved people with practical support, opportunities, empowerment, and sustainable solutions.

Our work places strong emphasis on children and education, youth empowerment, support for widows and single parents, livelihood opportunities, poverty reduction, and community development.

We believe lasting change is achieved when people are not only supported in times of need, but are also equipped with the knowledge, skills, resources, and partnerships needed to build a better future.

Vision & mission

What we are working toward

Our vision

A Liberia where vulnerable children, youth, families, and communities have access to education, opportunity, dignity, and the resources needed to thrive.

Our mission

To reach vulnerable and underserved people with education, empowerment, skills development, livelihood opportunities, and community-based support that transforms lives and contributes to sustainable development.

Core values

The commitments behind the work

Integrity

We promote honesty, accountability, transparency, and responsible stewardship.

Compassion

We serve people with dignity, respect, empathy, and care.

Empowerment

We seek to equip people to become self-reliant and active contributors to their communities.

Excellence

We pursue quality and measurable results in everything we do.

Partnership

We believe collaboration strengthens impact and creates sustainable solutions.

Sustainability

We focus on solutions that can continue creating value beyond immediate assistance.

Areas of focus

Where the foundation concentrates its work

Education & child development

Supporting vulnerable children to access quality education, school supplies, uniforms, learning materials, and opportunities for personal development.

Youth empowerment

Promoting education, skills development, employability, entrepreneurship, mentorship, and pathways to productive livelihoods.

Widows & single-parent support

Helping vulnerable families strengthen their economic capacity through empowerment, skills, livelihood initiatives, and targeted support.

Community development & poverty reduction

Working with communities to identify needs, strengthen local capacity, reduce vulnerability, and promote sustainable development.

Support for vulnerable and marginalized people

Providing compassionate, practical, and dignity-centered assistance to people facing significant social and economic challenges.

Current priority initiative

100 Children, One Greater Future

IRF is beginning its education sponsorship effort with a focused goal of supporting 100 children during the current school year, helping children from vulnerable backgrounds remain in school with the basic resources needed to learn with dignity.

The initiative represents the beginning of IRF’s broader education and empowerment journey. As capacity and partnerships grow, the Foundation intends to expand its reach and deepen the quality and sustainability of its programs.

Our approach to impact

Immediate support, joined to long-term capacity.

IRF believes that sustainable transformation requires more than one-time assistance. Our approach combines immediate support with empowerment, education, partnerships, accountability, and long-term capacity building.

We seek to listen to communities, identify practical needs, work with local stakeholders, mobilize resources responsibly, implement programs with measurable objectives, and learn from results to improve future interventions.

Partnership opportunities

Who we work with, and how.

ImpactReach Foundation welcomes meaningful partnerships with foundations, international organizations, corporations, government institutions, faith-based organizations, philanthropists, community leaders, and individuals who share a commitment to improving lives in Liberia.

Partnerships may include child sponsorship, education support, skills training, livelihood programs, community development projects, technical assistance, capacity building, in-kind support, and financial contributions.

Governance & accountability

Accountable to the people we serve.

IRF is committed to responsible governance, transparency, integrity, proper stewardship of resources, and accountability to beneficiaries, partners, donors, and the communities it serves.

The Foundation seeks to maintain appropriate organizational policies, documentation, financial controls, program records, and reporting practices as it grows.

ImpactReach Foundation is committed to being a trusted partner in community transformation—reaching people where vulnerability exists, creating opportunities where barriers exist, and building pathways toward a more hopeful and sustainable future.
