Responsible giving

Verify before you contribute.

Clear instructions, a defined purpose, and direct confirmation help protect both the donor and the communities IRF seeks to serve.

  1. 01

    Use an official contact

    Begin with an email address published on the IRF website.

  2. 02

    Request current instructions

    Do not rely on payment information forwarded from an unverified source.

  3. 03

    Confirm the purpose

    Clarify the program, initiative, or general mission area being supported.

  4. 04

    Keep a record

    Retain the written confirmation and any acknowledgment provided by IRF.

Donation questions

Know what to expect.

These answers reflect the information currently approved for the IRF website.

Can I enter card or bank details on this website?

No. The IRF website does not request or process card, bank, or mobile-money account details.

Can I donate immediately online?

No official online payment method is currently displayed. Contact IRF to request and verify the available donation process.

Can I choose a program to support?

You may identify a preferred program or initiative in your inquiry. IRF will confirm current needs and whether designated support can be accepted.

Can an organization make a donation?

Yes, an organization may begin a conversation about financial, in-kind, or partnership support and provide the relevant organizational details.

Can I donate goods or learning materials?

Contact IRF before sending anything so the need, quantity, condition, delivery plan, and intended use can be confirmed.
