About the launch

ImpactReach Foundation (IRF), a registered nonprofit organization in Liberia, is pleased to announce its official launch. The event marks the beginning of a renewed commitment to supporting vulnerable children, empowering families and young people, and promoting sustainable community development.

Our flagship initiative: 2,000 Children, One Greater Future

Through this initiative, IRF seeks to expand educational opportunities for vulnerable children. Individuals, churches, businesses, and organizations can support a child with a US$30 contribution, helping provide educational assistance and a brighter future.

Join us

We invite government institutions, development partners, churches, businesses, philanthropists, and the general public to join us in advancing this mission.
