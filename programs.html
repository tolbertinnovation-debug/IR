What we do

Five connected pathways to stronger futures.

IRF’s program direction responds to immediate barriers while keeping dignity, opportunity, and lasting community strength in view.

One mission, connected needs

People do not experience challenges one at a time.

Skills, livelihoods, recovery, family wellbeing, and child support are closely connected. IRF’s program areas reflect that reality.

Each area creates a clear place for communities and partners to begin a conversation. Current activity and availability should be confirmed directly with the foundation.
